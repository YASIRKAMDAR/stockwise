How to install

  1. Tap the download button above on the phone.
  2. When the download finishes, tap Open in the notification.
  3. If the phone says "For your security, your phone is not allowed to install unknown apps from this source", tap Settings, switch on Allow from this source, then go back.
  4. Tap Install, then Open.
  5. Allow the microphone when asked. That's what lets you talk to it.
